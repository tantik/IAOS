# IAOS Foundation v0.1 Draft

Status: Draft
Owner: Konstantin / Izumi IT
Purpose: Provide the practical foundation for how IAC builds, decides, documents, and improves.

## What IAOS is

IAOS is the internal operating system of IAC.

It is a Markdown-first source of truth for product and engineering workflows, AI-assisted development practices, decision records, documentation standards, reusable prompts, and company knowledge that should survive specific tools and AI providers.

IAC is the wider company and product ecosystem. It includes IAOS, ORUWA Business OS, OAES, current and future vertical SaaS products, product development processes, sales and marketing systems, market intelligence, onboarding, and the future AI Workforce.

## Why IAOS exists

IAC is currently founder-led and uses AI-assisted development to move quickly. IAOS exists to keep that speed practical, reviewable, and repeatable without adding bureaucracy.

IAOS should help IAC build real products now, reduce repeated decisions, reduce avoidable risk, keep context across AI sessions, and grow toward a scalable operating system over time.

## Current focus

The current business focus is IAC in the Japanese B2B market.

The first major SaaS platform is ORUWA Business OS, with Cafe Package as the current first vertical product focus.

## Repository structure

- `BOOT.md` - first-read operating context for humans and AI.
- `IAC_CONTEXT.md` - concise first-read overview of IAC, IAOS, ORUWA, OAES, and the current execution focus.
- `IAOS_SPECIFICATION.md` - definition of IAOS and what it is not.
- `IAOS_BLUEPRINT.md` - operating areas and repository map.
- `docs/` - strategic and operational IAC documents, including the master plan and current status.
- `foundation/` - North Star, current state, and transformation strategy.
- `workflows/` - practical repeatable workflows.
- `standards/` - standards that reduce repeated work or risk.
- `decisions/` - architecture and operating decision records.
- `prompts/` - reusable prompts for AI-assisted work.
- `archive/` - retired or historical material.

## How to use this repository

1. Start with `BOOT.md`.
2. Read `IAC_CONTEXT.md` for the current IAC framing.
3. Read the relevant foundation, workflow, standard, decision, or product documents.
4. Use the prompt templates when starting AI-assisted work.
5. Update IAOS when a repeated workflow, standard, or decision changes.
6. Keep documents concise and useful for current execution.

## Source of truth

This GitHub repository is the official source of truth for IAOS and the supporting IAC documentation.

Chat memory, project memory, local notes, and AI conversations may provide helpful context, but they are not official unless the relevant information is captured in this repository.

## Principle

Practical now, scalable later.

IAOS should support real product execution today while steadily growing toward the ideal long-term company operating system.
